PLL (Persian Language Learning) is a bilingual, interactive web platform designed for a general audience interested in learning the Persian language. Whether you are a complete beginner, a heritage speaker, or a linguistics enthusiast, PLL provides a structured and engaging journey through Persian's rich linguistic heritage.

History – Chronological exploration from Old Persian (Achaemenid inscriptions) through Middle Persian to Modern Persian and Dari.

Alphabet – Introduction to Persian script, letter shapes, connecting rules, and stroke order with bilingual guidance.

Sound System – Overview of Persian phonology, including vowels, consonants, stress patterns, and audio examples.

Grammar – Bilingual explanations of verb conjugation, word order (SOV), ezafe construction, and tense systems.

Vocabulary – Themed word lists with Persian script, transliteration, and English translations, filterable by dialect.

Stories – Short graded texts from folktales and literature to develop reading comprehension and cultural knowledge.

Flash Cards – Digital flashcards for memorizing letters, words, and phrases with spaced repetition and audio support.

Quiz – Interactive self-assessment across all sections with instant feedback, scores, and progress tracking.
