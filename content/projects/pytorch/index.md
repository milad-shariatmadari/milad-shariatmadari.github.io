The Iranian Languages Depository (ILD) is a collection of interactive
language distribution maps and linguistic maps of the Iranian languages
across Asia. The atlas is searchable and illustrates patterns across
different levels of grammar, including morphosyntax, lexical variation,
and typological features.

<!--more-->

The platform integrates annotated audio and video materials alongside
linguistic data, allowing users to explore language varieties through
interactive geographical visualization.

As an interactive atlas, ILD enables users to access the linguistic data
represented on the maps and encourages contributions, discussion, and
comments on the language data associated with each location. The project
supports collaborative research and digital documentation of Iranian
languages and dialects.
