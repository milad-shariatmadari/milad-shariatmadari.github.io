FLL (French Language Learning) is a bilingual, interactive web platform designed for a general audience interested in learning the French language. Whether you are a complete beginner, an intermediate learner, a heritage speaker, or a linguistics enthusiast, FLL provides a structured and engaging journey toward mastering modern French.

History – An introduction to the history and evolution of the French language, from its Latin origins through Old and Middle French to contemporary French.

Alphabet – Introduction to the French alphabet, letter names, spelling conventions, accents, ligatures, and pronunciation.

Sound System – Overview of French phonology, including vowels, nasal vowels, consonants, liaison, elision, intonation, and audio examples.

Grammar – Bilingual explanations of articles, noun gender, adjective agreement, verb conjugation, sentence structure, tenses, pronouns, and common grammatical patterns.

Vocabulary – Themed word lists with French words, pronunciation, English translations, and practical example sentences organized by proficiency level.

Dialogues – Short graded conversations and authentic texts to develop reading, listening, and conversational skills while introducing French-speaking cultures.

Flash Cards – Digital flashcards for memorizing vocabulary, expressions, verb conjugations, and grammar concepts with spaced repetition and audio support.

Quiz – Interactive self-assessment across all sections with instant feedback, scores, and progress tracking.
